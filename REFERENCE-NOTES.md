# Current direction after V3 review

The untouched prototype remains in `reference/drewneilson-homepage-v3.html`.

Preserve the cream palette, rust accents, serif headlines, and varied story sizes. The first map overemphasized abstract thematic links and conflated jobs with stories and concepts. The revision uses three explicit types: job (work context), story (an account from that work), and thought experiment (concept, sketch, or playtested idea).

The homepage separates work stories from thought experiments. Job descriptions live in content files and feed About, individual pages, and the map. Authorship and development labels remain visible.

The map uses a quiet canvas with short labels, distinct node sizes/colors, panning, zoom controls, and a side profile. Five job-to-story relationships establish context. Six direct piece-to-piece links remain from V3. Thematic lenses are archived as editorial reference, rather than drawn as nodes. Relationships are recorded once and navigable in either direction.

This is an implementation for review, not a claim that the content taxonomy or map now meets the user's taste. No Cloudflare publication has occurred. Original claims and attribution still need editorial review before publication.
