## The first decision

In this fictional pilot, the entrance offered several competing invitations before visitors had established where they were.

> The useful question was not “What else should we explain?” but “Which decision can wait?”

## The attempt

We sketched the arrival sequence, made a temporary sign, and asked colleagues to walk through the route without coaching.

## The limit

A clearer starting point was promising, but this sample does not claim a measured improvement. Accessibility instructions still needed to remain explicit.
