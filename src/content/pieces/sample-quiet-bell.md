## The assumption

A soft sound might have invited people to pause at an entrance. In this fictional trial, it instead competed with conversation and made the arrival feel managed.

## Stopping was an outcome

We stopped the trial rather than treating completion as success. The useful result was identifying a constraint: an invitation should be easy to decline.

There is no launch, growth claim, or success metric hidden behind the project title.
