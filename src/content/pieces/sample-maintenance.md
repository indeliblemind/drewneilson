## After the handover

A guide or service does not stop changing when its first version is delivered. Someone still has to notice outdated information, replace worn parts, and resolve ambiguous ownership.

This fictional idea connects repair documentation with a later shared ledger. It is a subject worth discussing independently, not a separate record for every anecdote about maintenance.
