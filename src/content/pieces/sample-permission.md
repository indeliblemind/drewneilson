## A useful boundary

If someone cannot comfortably decline, the invitation may function as a demand.

The fictional Quiet Bell trial and unbuilt Signal Lamp offer different ways to examine this question. Neither settles it.

## What remains uncertain

A clear opt-out can help, but it does not automatically remove social pressure.
