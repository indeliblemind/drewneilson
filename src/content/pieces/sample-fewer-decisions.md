## The proposition

A person may need fewer competing signals rather than more instructions.

This fictional idea emerged from the Welcome Route and informed the Repair Cards. The direction of those links matters: the later guide did not cause the earlier entrance trial.

## A qualification

Removing guidance can exclude people. The aim is to defer unnecessary decisions while keeping essential information available.
