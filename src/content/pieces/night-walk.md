## The invitation

A short route used light, sound, and printed clues to offer several paths through a neighborhood. Two informal fictional trials explored how much explanation participants wanted before starting.

The project led to a question about thresholds: when does uncertainty become inviting rather than confusing?

## Current stage

A tested prototype, not a completed public program. This distinction belongs in the status, not in the node type.
