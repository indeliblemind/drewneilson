## The observation

People do not always need another explanation. They may need fewer competing signals or an easier first action.

This fictional observation emerged from the Wayfinding pilot, then informed the Field Guide. The same idea can connect work across different jobs without merging those jobs.

## A useful limit

Subtraction is not automatically helpful. Removing an essential instruction can exclude someone. The question is which uncertainty a person needs resolved to take their next step.
