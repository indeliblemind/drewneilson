**Smash\*It** started as a bet: that people would pay to break things in a controlled environment. It sounded ridiculous, which is exactly why I liked it.

We opened in Denver with a handful of breakables, some safety gear, and no real playbook. What we quickly learned is that people weren’t just coming to destroy stuff—they were coming to **perform**. It was less about anger and more about the rare opportunity to be loud, messy, and completely unselfconscious in front of people who were cheering them on.
