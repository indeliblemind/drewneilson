## An artifact that stays with the object

These fictional cards recorded a repair’s purpose, the parts used, and the warning signs to watch for next time.

### An example card

1. Identify the symptom.
2. Record the repair decision.
3. Explain what would require another inspection.

The cards were a production artifact, not a theory of every repair. Their wording needed to work for someone who had not been in the workshop.
