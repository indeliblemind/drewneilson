## Keeping a service usable

This fictional workshop repaired household objects and helped owners understand what could be repaired next time. My role crossed scheduling, tools, instructions, and the business model.

## Two kinds of work

The [Repair Cards](/pieces/sample-repair-cards/) made individual repairs easier to explain. The [Handoff Ledger](/pieces/sample-handoff-ledger/) addressed what happened between shifts.

Neither project was the whole job. The ordinary work was keeping the service dependable while making room to improve it.
